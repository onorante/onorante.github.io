Short bio

Luca Onorante heads the Forecasting group at the Joint Research Centre of the European Commission and co-leads the SETI (Socio-economic & territorial impact) portfolio.

He was Senior Economist at the European Central Bank until 2019. From 2013 to 2016 he worked at the Central Bank of Ireland as Deputy Head of Research, Head of the Macro Modelling Project and Head of Monetary Policy, and in 2015 and 2016 he was a member of the Eurosystem/ESCB Monetary Policy Committee. He was an Economist at the ECB from 2003 to 2013. Earlier, he was a research assistant at the European University Institute and a consultant at UNCTAD.

He has taught macroeconomics at the University of Bolzano and advanced macroeconomics (master's level) at Trinity College Dublin. He holds a PhD from the European University Institute.

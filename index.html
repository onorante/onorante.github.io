Luca Onorante

Deputy Head of Unit, Joint Research Centre, European Commission

Directorate T · Digital Transformation and Data · Ispra, Italy

Luca Onorante is a macroeconomist at the European Commission's Joint Research Centre (JRC).

His research covers macroeconomic forecasting and nowcasting, Bayesian econometrics, distributional analysis, and models that link housing, consumption and climate. Before joining the JRC he was an economist and senior economist at the European Central Bank, and headed the Monetary Policy Division of the Central Bank of Ireland.

Current work

EconLab

A cross-directorate JRC infrastructure that makes economic models and data AI-ready for EU policy analysis, with pilots on EUROMOD and RHOMOLO.

Europe Inequality Observatory

A preparatory action developing new methodologies to measure and assess inequalities across Europe.
